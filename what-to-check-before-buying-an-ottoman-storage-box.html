What to Check Before Buying an Ottoman Storage Box

An ottoman storage box can provide useful extra space without taking up an entire wardrobe or chest of drawers. Before buying one, check its dimensions, capacity, construction and how it will work in your bedroom.

Start With the Available Space

The first thing to check is where the storage box will actually be placed. Measuring the available floor space before buying helps prevent a storage solution from becoming an obstruction.

Think about the space around the box as well as its footprint. You should still be able to move around the bedroom comfortably and access nearby furniture without the storage box getting in the way.

Measure before buying:
  • Available floor width
  • Available floor depth
  • Available height
  • Clearance around nearby furniture
  • Access through doors and hallways

Check the Storage Box Dimensions

External dimensions tell you how much space the box will occupy, but they do not necessarily tell you exactly how much storage is available inside.

Check the product specifications carefully and consider whether the internal space will suit the items you want to store.

A storage box can be useful for bedding, blankets, cushions, clothing and other bedroom essentials, but the best size depends on what you actually need to put away.

Think About What You Will Store

Storage is most useful when the box matches the type of items you need to organise.

An ottoman storage box can be particularly useful for items that are not needed every day, such as spare bedding, seasonal clothing, blankets, towels or cushions.

For bedding

Check whether the internal space is large enough for folded duvets, sheets or spare blankets without forcing them into the box.

For seasonal items

A separate storage box can keep winter clothing or other seasonal bedroom items out of everyday wardrobe space.

Consider the Construction

The construction and finish matter because the storage box will be used regularly. Look at the materials, upholstery and overall design rather than choosing purely on appearance.

If the box is upholstered, check the available fabric or finish options and choose something that works with the rest of the bedroom.

Check the Height Carefully

Height can be particularly important if the storage box will sit beneath or beside other bedroom furniture.

Measure the surrounding area and consider whether the box will interfere with skirting boards, wardrobes, drawers or other furniture.

The height can also affect how convenient the storage box is to use. Consider whether you will be able to place and remove items comfortably.

Think About How Often You Will Use It

The right storage box depends partly on how frequently you need access to the contents.

If you are storing seasonal bedding or clothing, the box may only need occasional access. If you plan to store everyday items, accessibility becomes more important.

Ask yourself:
  • What will I store inside it?
  • How often will I need those items?
  • Where will the box be positioned?
  • Can I access it easily?
  • Does the size suit the room?

Match the Finish to the Bedroom

Storage does not have to look purely functional. An upholstered storage box can become part of the overall bedroom design.

Consider the colour and fabric finish alongside your existing bed, headboard, curtains and other furniture. A complementary finish can make the storage box feel like part of the room rather than an additional item.

Check Access Before Positioning It

Before placing the storage box permanently, make sure there is enough space to move it into position and access it when required.

This is especially important in smaller bedrooms where every part of the floor plan needs to work efficiently.

Ottoman Storage Box Buying Checklist

Check these points before ordering:
  • External dimensions
  • Available storage capacity
  • What you intend to store
  • Available floor space
  • Height and surrounding clearance
  • Fabric or finish
  • Colour options
  • Access through the room and doorway

The Bottom Line

The best ottoman storage box is one that fits both your room and your storage needs. Start by measuring the available space, then consider the box's dimensions, construction, finish and intended use.

Taking those details into account before buying makes it much easier to choose a storage solution that remains useful rather than simply taking up additional floor space.
