How Do Ottoman Bed Gas Lift Systems Work?

Gas lift mechanisms make it easier to raise an ottoman bed base and access the storage space underneath. Here is how the system works and what to consider when choosing an ottoman bed.

Quick answer: A gas lift system uses gas struts to assist the movement of the hinged bed base. The mechanism helps support the lifting action so the storage compartment can be accessed without having to lift the entire base unaided.

What is a gas lift ottoman bed?

An ottoman bed is designed with storage beneath the mattress area. Instead of opening drawers from the side, the upper part of the bed lifts to reveal the storage compartment underneath.

On a gas lift design, the lifting mechanism assists this movement. This makes the large hinged base more practical to raise and lower during normal use.

How the gas struts help

Gas struts are positioned as part of the lifting mechanism. As the bed base is raised, the struts assist the movement and help support the lifted platform.

The exact construction can vary between bed designs, so it is important to follow the manufacturer's instructions for the specific model rather than assuming every mechanism works identically.

  • The base is hinged to provide access to the storage area.
  • Gas struts assist the lifting movement.
  • The mechanism helps support the raised base.
  • The mattress normally remains on the lifting platform.
  • Always follow the supplied assembly and usage instructions.

Why gas lift can be useful

The main advantage is accessibility. A full-height storage compartment can be opened from above without requiring clearance beside the bed for a drawer.

This can be particularly useful in bedrooms where floor space is limited or where conventional storage furniture would make the room feel crowded.

Does the mattress stay on the bed?

Ottoman bed lifting systems are designed around the complete bed setup, with the mattress positioned on the lifting platform. The mechanism is intended to assist access to the storage area beneath that platform.

Because designs vary, always check the specifications and operating instructions for the particular bed before use.

How to use the mechanism safely

The lifting mechanism should be operated according to the manufacturer's instructions. Avoid forcing the base or placing unnecessary loads on parts of the mechanism that are not designed for them.

When lowering the platform, keep hands, bedding and other objects clear of moving sections. This is especially important when children are around the bed.

What should you check before buying?

  • Check how the lifting mechanism operates.
  • Read the manufacturer's assembly instructions.
  • Consider how frequently you will access the storage.
  • Check the bed size and available bedroom space.
  • Consider the amount of storage you actually need.
  • Make sure the surrounding room allows comfortable use.

Final thoughts

Gas lift systems make the large lifting section of an ottoman bed considerably more practical by assisting the movement of the hinged base. The result is easy access to a large area of under-bed storage without relying on side-opening drawers.

When choosing an ottoman bed, look beyond the mechanism itself. Consider the overall construction, storage capacity, room layout and how frequently you expect to use the storage.
