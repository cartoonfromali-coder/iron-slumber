Divan Bed Buying Checklist

A practical checklist for comparing divan beds before you buy, from dimensions and storage to mattress compatibility and everyday bedroom use.

1. Check the Overall Size

Start by checking both the mattress size and the complete external dimensions of the divan. The base footprint is what you need when planning the bed around existing furniture.

Measure the available bedroom space and check how the bed will work alongside wardrobes, bedside furniture and other fixed features.

2. Decide Whether You Need Storage

Storage can be useful when bedroom space is limited, but it is not automatically necessary for every room.

Think about whether you need somewhere for spare bedding, clothing, blankets or other bedroom items. If storage matters, consider which drawer position will remain accessible after the bed is installed.

3. Consider the Headboard Style

A headboard can have a major effect on the overall appearance of a divan. Consider its height, shape and upholstery style alongside the furniture already in the bedroom.

The best choice should complement the rest of the room rather than making the bed feel disconnected from the existing design.

4. Check Mattress Requirements

If you are buying a divan and mattress together, check the available mattress options and make sure the selected mattress matches the required size.

Also consider the sleeping feel you prefer. Support and firmness can have a significant effect on how comfortable the finished bed feels.

5. Look at the Base Construction

The base is an important part of the complete bed. Look at the construction and specifications rather than judging the divan purely by its appearance.

If you are comparing several beds, check the details of each configuration so you know exactly what is included.

6. Check Access Around the Bed

Think about how you will move around the finished bed. Check the position of doors, wardrobes, windows and other furniture before ordering.

If the bed includes drawers, make sure there is enough clear space to open them properly. Good bedroom planning can make storage much more useful.

7. Review the Available Configuration

Before completing the purchase, review all available options carefully. Depending on the bed, these can include:

  • Bed size
  • Mattress options
  • Storage configuration
  • Headboard style
  • Upholstery colour
  • Assembly options

8. Measure the Delivery Route

The bedroom is not the only space that needs to be considered. Check the route from the entrance to the room where the bed will be installed.

Measure narrow doorways, staircases, corridors and other restricted areas where necessary. Planning access beforehand can make delivery considerably easier.

Quick Buying Checklist

  • Measure the available bedroom space.
  • Check the complete external bed dimensions.
  • Decide whether storage is required.
  • Check drawer access if storage is included.
  • Choose a suitable headboard style.
  • Check mattress size and options.
  • Review upholstery and colour choices.
  • Check assembly requirements.
  • Measure the delivery route.

Final Thoughts

A divan bed should be considered as part of the whole bedroom rather than as an isolated piece of furniture. Checking dimensions, storage, mattress options, access and configuration before buying can help you avoid expensive or inconvenient mistakes.
