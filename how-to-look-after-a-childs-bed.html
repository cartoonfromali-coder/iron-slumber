How to Look After a Child's Bed

Regular care can help keep a child's bed looking clean and working as intended. From checking the frame to caring for upholstery, these simple habits can make everyday maintenance easier.

Start With the Manufacturer's Care Instructions

The most important rule is to follow the care instructions supplied with the specific bed. Different materials and finishes can require different cleaning methods.

Avoid applying cleaning products or treatments to upholstered surfaces unless they are suitable for the material and recommended by the manufacturer.

Keep the Bed Frame Clean

Dust and everyday bedroom debris can collect around the frame, particularly underneath the bed and around joints or storage areas.

Regular gentle cleaning can help keep the bed looking tidy. For upholstered beds, use the cleaning method recommended for the specific fabric rather than treating the surface like a hard frame.

A simple routine can include:
  • Removing visible dust regularly
  • Cleaning accessible areas underneath the bed
  • Keeping storage compartments organised
  • Following the recommended upholstery care method

Check the Frame From Time to Time

It is sensible to periodically inspect the bed frame, particularly after assembly or if the bed has been moved.

Look for anything that appears loose, damaged or out of place. If the bed requires tightening or adjustment, follow the manufacturer's instructions rather than modifying the construction yourself.

Protect Upholstered Surfaces

Upholstered children's beds can add a softer, more decorative finish to the bedroom, but the fabric should be treated according to its care requirements.

Deal with spills promptly using the recommended cleaning approach and avoid aggressive scrubbing that could affect the fabric.

Keep Storage Areas Organised

If the bed includes storage, avoid filling compartments beyond what the manufacturer recommends. Keeping stored items organised also makes it easier to clean around the bed.

Use storage for suitable bedroom items and make sure drawers or compartments can open and close normally without obstruction.

Look After the Mattress Too

The mattress is a major part of the sleeping setup and should be maintained according to its own care instructions.

Do not treat mattress care as part of the bed-frame cleaning routine. Check the mattress manufacturer's recommendations for rotation, protection, cleaning and ventilation.

What Should You Avoid?

Avoid these common mistakes:
  • Using unsuitable cleaning products on upholstery
  • Ignoring loose or damaged components
  • Overloading storage compartments
  • Dragging the bed unnecessarily when moving it
  • Ignoring the manufacturer's care instructions
  • Using the bed outside its intended purpose

A Simple Maintenance Routine

You do not need an elaborate routine to look after a child's bed. Regular cleaning and occasional checks are usually the most practical approach.

Regularly

Keep the visible frame and surrounding area clean and deal with spills according to the appropriate care instructions.

Occasionally

Check the frame, storage areas and any accessible fittings for signs that something needs attention.

When moving the bed

Handle the bed carefully and follow any disassembly or moving instructions supplied with the product.

The Bottom Line

Looking after a child's bed mainly comes down to regular cleaning, sensible use and following the manufacturer's instructions.

Keeping the frame, upholstery, storage areas and mattress properly maintained can help the whole sleeping setup remain practical and presentable as the bedroom continues to be used.
