What to Check in a Kids Bed Frame

A children's bed needs to work as part of the whole bedroom. Before buying, consider the frame construction, size, support, storage and how the bed will fit into everyday use.

Start With the Bed Frame Construction

The frame is the foundation of the bed, so construction should be one of the first things you consider. Look at how the frame is designed and whether it is intended for regular everyday use.

A children's bed can experience plenty of movement beyond simply sleeping. Children may climb into bed, change position frequently and use the surrounding bedroom space throughout the day. A practical frame should therefore suit normal family use.

Check the Overall Dimensions

Do not judge a kids bed only by the mattress size. The external dimensions of the complete bed can be larger, particularly when the design includes an upholstered headboard or other structural elements.

Before buying, measure:
  • The available floor space
  • The full width of the bed
  • The full length of the bed
  • Clearance around the bed
  • Space needed for drawers or other storage features

Think About the Sleeping Surface

The mattress size must match the bed frame. Check the recommended mattress dimensions before buying so that the sleeping surface fits the frame correctly.

If you are replacing an existing children's bed, check the current mattress dimensions rather than assuming that every bed described as a single will have identical external dimensions.

Consider Storage From the Beginning

Storage can be useful in a child's bedroom because floor space is often shared between sleeping, playing and studying.

However, storage should not be the only reason to choose a particular bed. Think about what you actually need to store and whether drawers or another storage arrangement will be practical in the available space.

Check Access Around the Bed

A bed may fit the room on paper but still be awkward to use if there is not enough practical space around it.

Think about where doors open, where wardrobes and drawers are positioned and how easily the child can get in and out of bed. Also consider whether the bed's storage features need additional clearance to open.

Look at the Headboard and Upholstery

An upholstered children's bed can change the overall feel of the room while also adding a softer visual element around the sleeping area.

When comparing upholstered designs, consider the shape of the headboard, the available fabric choices and how the finish will work with the rest of the bedroom.

Kids Bed Frame Checklist

Check these points before ordering:
  • Full external bed dimensions
  • Compatible mattress size
  • Frame construction
  • Available storage options
  • Clearance around the bed
  • Headboard design
  • Fabric or finish options
  • Delivery access to the bedroom

The Bottom Line

The best kids bed frame is not simply the one that looks good. It should fit the available room, accommodate the correct mattress, provide suitable support and work with the way the bedroom is actually used.

Taking a few measurements and checking the complete specifications before ordering can make the buying process much easier.
